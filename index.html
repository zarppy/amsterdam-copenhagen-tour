A Curated Journey Through

Netherlands & Belgium

27 September – 9 October 2026

PW-tour EU EDITION
00

Departure: Melbourne to Bangkok

Sunday, 27 September

13:30
Flight TG466 departs MEL

Begin the journey to Europe via Bangkok.

20:00
Arrival BKK & Transit Stay

Check into Port Canary Airport Hotel for a quick rest.

01

Arrival Amsterdam & Jordaan

Monday, 28 September

05:35 - 12:40
Flight TG936 to AMS

Early departure from BKK. Arrive at Schiphol Airport (AMS) midday.

14:00
Ruby Emma Check-in

Taxi to Ruby Emma Hotel (near Spaklerweg). High-tech modern comfort.

16:00
Jordaan Pit Stops

Grab lunch at Gifu Ramen Bar. Stock up on water at Albert Heijn. Try the snack wall at Febo.

Stay: Ruby Emma Hotel (1)

02

Zaandam & Iconic Dutch Charm

Tuesday, 29 September

09:00 - 12:00
Zaandam Morning

Capture the Inntel Hotel architecture. Visit the windmills and wooden boot workshops at Zaanse Schans.

14:00 - 16:30
Damrak & Central Stroll

Walk the Damrak "Dancing Houses" and explore Dam Square.

Stay: Ruby Emma Hotel (2)

03

Museums & Botanical Calm

Wednesday, 30 September

08:30 - 12:30
The Big Two Museums

Complete visits to the Van Gogh Museum and Rijksmuseum before lunch.

14:30 - 16:30
Hortus Botanicus

Relax in one of the world's oldest botanical gardens, Hortus Botanicus.

20:00
Canal Night Silhouette

Recommended: Board a shared canal boat at Damrak (Lovers/Centraal) for a family-friendly view.

Stay: Ruby Emma Hotel (3)

04

Giethoorn Loop & Utrecht

Thursday, 1 October

08:00
Car Rental Pickup (AMS)

Pick up your rental car. Drive north to Giethoorn.

10:30 - 13:00
Giethoorn Exploration

Park at P1 (Binnenpad). Take a boat route (Yellow/Red). Quick photo snap at Vollenhove harbor nearby.

14:00 - 15:30
Zwolle (Optional)

Quick photo snap at the medieval Sassenpoort gate if time allows.

16:30 - 18:00
Utrecht Pit Stop

Visit Firma Stroop for fresh stroopwafels. Explore the canals of Utrecht.

Stay: Hotel De Roode Schuur (Nijkerk).

05

Architecture Sprint: Rotterdam & The Hague

Friday, 2 October

09:00 - 11:30
Rotterdam Markthal

Architecture sprint at the horseshoe hall. Visit the Cube Houses nearby.

12:30 - 15:00
The Hague (Den Haag)

Visit the Binnenhof (political heart) and the Peace Palace. A perfect architectural bridge before Belgium.

18:00 - 20:00
Brussels Arrival

Drive south (1.5 hrs). Check into Hotel Avenue Louise. Dinner in the Louise district.

Stay: Hotel Avenue Louise (Brussels).

06

Brussels Deep Dive

Saturday, 3 October

09:00 - 12:00
Atomium & Waffles

Morning at the Atomium. Treat yourself to a legendary waffle at Maison Dandoy.

14:00 - 17:00
Grand Place & Frites

Explore the historic Grand Place. Quick stop at Frites Atelier for the best Belgian fries.

Stay: Hotel Avenue Louise (Brussels).

07

Ghent Charm

Sunday, 4 October

10:00 - 14:00
Ghent Graslei

Drive to Ghent. Walk the Graslei waterfront and capture the reflections of the historic guildhalls.

16:00
Castelnou Check-in

Check into Castelnou Aparthotel. Evening at leisure in Ghent's medieval streets.

Stay: Castelnou Aparthotel (Ghent).

08

Bruges Arrival

Monday, 5 October

10:00 - 13:00
Drive to Bruges

Morning drive to the medieval crown jewel. Check into Hotel Bourgoensch Hof, located right on the water.

15:00 - 18:00
Bruges Hot Chocolate

Visit The Old Chocolate House for a must-try hot chocolate experience.

Stay: Hotel Bourgoensch Hof (Bruges).

09

Bruges Immersion

Tuesday, 6 October

09:00 - 12:00
Canal & Bridges

Visit Minnewater Lake and the Lover's Bridge. A full day to soak in the atmosphere.

13:00 - 15:00
Boho Brunch

Enjoy a relaxed meal at Boho Brunch or explore the local market square.

Stay: Hotel Bourgoensch Hof (Bruges).

10

Antwerp Station

Wednesday, 7 October

10:00 - 13:00
Antwerp Railway Cathedral

Drive to Antwerp. Visit the world-famous Antwerp Central Station. A quick architecture sprint.

16:00
Holiday Inn Check-in

Check into Holiday Inn Express Antwerp. Explore the diamond district nearby.

Stay: Holiday Inn Express Antwerp.

11

Final Amsterdam Sprint

Thursday, 8 October

10:00 - 13:00
Jordaan Bucket List

Morning in the 9 Streets. Grab the legendary cookies at Van Stapele and the best fries at Fabel Friet.

14:00 - 17:30
Leidsestraat & Rembrant

Final shopping and a quick pasta at Harry's Amsterdam. Visit the Rembrandt Corner for a last coffee.

18:30
Schiphol Transfer

Head to the airport for check-in. Flight BR76 departs at 21:40 for Bangkok.
