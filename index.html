A Curated Journey Through

Netherlands & Belgium

27 September – 9 October 2026

PW-tour EU EDITION
00

Departure: Melbourne to Bangkok

Sunday, 27 September

13:30
Flight TG466 departs MEL

Begin the journey to Europe via Bangkok.

20:00
Arrival BKK & Transit Stay

Check into Port Canary Airport Hotel for a quick rest.

01

Arrival Amsterdam & Jordaan

Monday, 28 September

05:35 - 12:40
Flight TG936 to AMS

Early departure from BKK. Arrive at Schiphol Airport (AMS) midday.

14:00
Ruby Emma Check-in

Taxi to Ruby Emma Hotel (near Spaklerweg). High-tech modern comfort.

16:00
Jordaan Pit Stops

Grab lunch at Gifu Ramen Bar. Stock up on water at Albert Heijn. Try the snack wall at Febo.

Stay: Ruby Emma Hotel (1)

02

Zaandam & Iconic Dutch Charm

Tuesday, 29 September

09:00 - 12:00
Zaandam Morning

Capture the Inntel Hotel architecture. Visit the windmills and wooden boot workshops at Zaanse Schans.

14:00 - 16:30
Damrak & Central Stroll

Walk the Damrak "Dancing Houses" and explore Dam Square.

Stay: Ruby Emma Hotel (2)

03

Museums & Botanical Calm

Wednesday, 30 September

08:30 - 12:30
The Big Two Museums

Complete visits to the Van Gogh Museum and Rijksmuseum before lunch.

14:30 - 16:30
Hortus Botanicus

Relax in one of the world's oldest botanical gardens, Hortus Botanicus.

20:00
Canal Night Silhouette

Recommended: Board a shared canal boat at Damrak (Lovers/Centraal) for a family-friendly view.

Stay: Ruby Emma Hotel (3)

04

Giethoorn Loop & Utrecht

Thursday, 1 October

08:00
Car Rental Pickup (AMS)

Pick up your rental car. Drive north to Giethoorn.

10:30 - 13:00
Giethoorn Exploration

Park at P1 (Binnenpad). Take a boat route (Yellow/Red). Quick photo snap at Vollenhove harbor nearby.

14:00 - 15:30
Zwolle (Optional)

Quick photo snap at the medieval Sassenpoort gate if time allows.

16:30 - 18:00
Utrecht Pit Stop

Visit Firma Stroop for fresh stroopwafels. Explore the canals of Utrecht.

Stay: Hotel De Roode Schuur (Nijkerk).

05

Rotterdam & night-Brussels

Friday, 2 October

09:00 - 12:00
Rotterdam Markthal

Architecture sprint at the horseshoe hall. Visit the Cube Houses nearby.

15:00 - 17:00
Drive to Brussels

Direct drive south (2 hrs). Check into Adagio Grand Place.

19:00 - 21:00
Dinner & Chocolate

Classic mussels at Chez Leon followed by a visit to the historic Neuhaus chocolate shop.

Stay: Adagio Grand Place (Brussels).

06

Brussels Hub (Fast-Paced)

Saturday, 3 October

06:30 - 08:30
Sunrise at Atomium

Beat the crowds for empty square shots of the iconic spheres.

10:00 - 12:00
Waffle & Fries Morning

Enjoy a legendary waffle at Maison Dandoy and premium fries at Frites Atelier.

14:00 - 16:00
Brussels Art & History

Explore the Sablon district or the Royal Palace. Afternoon coffee at Frank Cafe.

19:30 - 21:00
Mont des Arts Night Silhouette

Capture the stunning city skyline at dusk.

Stay: Adagio Grand Place (Brussels).

07

Scenic Meuse (Namur & Dinant)

Sunday, 4 October

09:00 - 13:00
Namur & Dinant

Drive to Namur Citadel. Continue to Dinant for the iconic river reflection shot.

20:00 - 21:00
Dinant Night Illumination

The Citadel and church reflected in the Meuse. Breathtaking at night.

Stay: Château de Namur. Countryside charm with estate views.

08

Ghent & night-Bruges

Monday, 5 October

08:00 - 12:00
Ghent Morning Walk

Morning coffee at Le Pain Quotidien. Walk the Graslei and visit Choccoladebar Mayana.

15:00 - 17:00
Bruges Arrival

Afternoon in Bruges. Must try hot chocolate at The Old Chocolate House and brunch at Boho Brunch.

20:30 - 22:00
Bruges Night Fairytale

Rozenhoedkaai (Rosary Quay) illuminated. Pure magic.

Stay: Grand Hotel Casselbergh Bruges.

09

Bruges Depth & Antwerp

Tuesday, 6 October

09:00 - 12:00
Bruges Final Icons

Visit the Minnewater Lake and the Lover's Bridge. Quick lunch at Nakhon Thai.

15:00 - 18:00
Antwerp Arrival

Drive to Antwerp. Explore the MAS Museum or the fashion district.

Stay: Hotel Franq Antwerp.

10

Antwerp & AMS Return

Wednesday, 7 October

09:00 - 12:00
Antwerp Railway Cathedral

Visit the world-famous Antwerp Central Station. Architecture sprint before the drive.

14:00 - 17:00
Drive to Amsterdam

2.5 hour drive north. Return to the familiar canals.

19:00
Car Return (Schiphol)

Return the rental car and check into Hotel Mercier (Jordaan) for the final night.

Stay: Hotel Mercier (AMS).

11

Final Amsterdam Sprint

Thursday, 8 October

10:00 - 13:00
Jordaan Bucket List

Morning in the 9 Streets. Grab the legendary cookies at Van Stapele and the best fries at Fabel Friet.

14:00 - 17:30
Leidsestraat & Rembrant

Final shopping and a quick pasta at Harry's Amsterdam. Visit the Rembrandt Corner for a last coffee.

18:30
Schiphol Transfer

Head to the airport for check-in. Flight BR76 departs at 21:40 for Bangkok.

Essential Travel Tips

  • Brussels: Grand Place, Atomium
  • Ghent: Graslei Waterfront
  • Bruges: Rozenhoedkaai (Dawn)
  • Dinant: Riverside Reflection
